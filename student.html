Citation Managers

Citation managers help keep track of sources, research papers, and citations so you do not have to organize everything manually.

  • Zotero — My personal favorite. It is free, easy to learn, and can often connect directly to papers and PDFs so you can keep everything together in one place.
  • Mendeley — Helpful if you like highlighting and taking notes directly on PDFs. It is also useful for group projects and shared papers.
  • EndNote — A more advanced citation manager often used by universities and long-term research projects. It can feel overwhelming at first, but some researchers really love its organization tools.
  • Citavi — Good for people who want to organize notes, quotes, ideas, and references all together while researching.
